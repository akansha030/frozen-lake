# Frozen lake

An interaction exploration. A frozen lake seen from above. Tap to crack it, drag to score it, hold still and let the pressure build. Fish drift under the ice as soft shadows at three speeds. Hit one three times (it darts after every hit) and it bursts up through the cracks and flies into the counter top-right. Every so often a fast school passes; those take one tap each. Sound is synthesised in the browser; drop an audio file on the page to use your own ambience.

Single file, no build: open `index.html`.
