# Frozen lake

An interaction exploration. A frozen lake seen from above. Tap to crack it, drag to score it, hold still and let the pressure build. Fish drift under the ice as soft shadows: hit one three times (it darts after every hit) and the ice breaks open to let it out. The counter top-right keeps score. Sound is synthesised in the browser; drop an audio file on the page to use your own ambience.

Single file, no build: open `index.html`.
